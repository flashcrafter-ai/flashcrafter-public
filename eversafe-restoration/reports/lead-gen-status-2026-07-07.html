Client Update Draft

Chad, quick status update building on what we sent June 30: the site is still not at zero. For June 9-July 6, Google Search Console shows 4,159 search impressions and 12 organic clicks, up from 2,159 impressions and 8 clicks in the previous 28 days. The most recent week also improved to an average position of 20.3.

The honest read is the same: Google is testing the site, but most rankings are still below the top results, so lead volume is still early. The strongest early traction is around Chesterfield searches like water damage restoration, mold remediation, flood cleanup, and mold removal. The biggest next lever is still the Google Business Profile verification/profile cleanup, because that heavily affects local map visibility and calls.

On our side, the next actions are to verify lead tracking for calls/forms and keep pushing the pages that are already gaining impressions. Once tracking and GBP are stable, we can make a clearer decision on whether to add Google Ads for faster lead volume.
