Alignment verdict

This is strongly aligned with the Growth Brief direction. It preserves owner-facing simplicity while giving admins and agents a reusable scoring contract for every section.

What changes in V2

Reviews and Website use score-to-multiplier logic. SEO/Maps and Ads use service-location economic math, with scores explaining readiness, gaps, and opportunity. Directories / LSA stay blocked until verified.

Boundary to keep

Never turn a low visibility score directly into low jobs. Low visibility can mean high upside when search demand, economics, and eligibility support it.
