Improvement Opportunities

Keyword quality and spend control

Some higher-volume drywall searches are producing leads, but they also carry higher average click costs or lower quality scores. The practical next step is to improve ad and landing-page alignment around the strongest drywall repair terms, while reducing wasted spend on searches that have clicked without converting.

Area Evidence Client-safe next step
High-volume core terms "sheetrock repair service" and "drywall installation service" produced 15 combined leads. Keep coverage active and continue improving ad relevance for these services.
Higher-cost near-me terms "drywallers near me" and "drywall contractors near me" converted, but at $130.13 and $125.16 per lead. Review bids, ad copy, and landing-page match before scaling these terms further.
Clicked terms with no leads "drywall repair columbus ohio" spent $85.84 with 4 clicks and no tracked leads in this period. Add negatives or tighten matching where searches are not producing leads.

Recommended Next Steps

Suggested agenda for the next client check-in

  • Review whether the 25 tracked leads match what GLZ saw by phone, form, and text.
  • Confirm which drywall lead types are most valuable: repair, installation, ceiling repair, water damage, or sheetrock work.
  • Approve keyword cleanup so spend moves away from non-converting searches and toward proven repair terms.
  • Decide whether to keep the current drywall campaign as the main focus before expanding other service campaigns.
